# Clurio Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Clurio is a hidden-object puzzle game. We built it to be played without an account, and we do not collect your personal data.

## What the game stores

Your player name, level progress, coins, badges, rankings and (if you choose one) your profile photo are saved only on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

## What we don't do

Clurio has no accounts, sign-in or passwords. This version has no ads and no in-app purchases. We do not use analytics or tracking, we do not sell data, and we do not share data with third parties.

## Photos

If you add a profile photo, the game asks for permission to access your photos. The photo is kept on your device only and is never uploaded.

## Deleting your data

Go to Profile > Delete my data to erase everything Clurio has saved. Uninstalling the game also deletes all its data.

## Children

Clurio is family-friendly and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a future update adds features such as ads or purchases, we will update this policy and the date above before those features go live.
